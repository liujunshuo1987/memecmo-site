Choosing a GEO provider in Vietnam

Ask an AI assistant who to hire for GEO in Vietnam and, after naming a few agencies, it gives the same advice in English, Vietnamese and Chinese: do not sign on a ranking — have the candidates measure the same questions, then compare. These are those tests, and how we meet each one.

Why the first answer is decided off your website

Across the 12 brands we measure in Vietnam (3,011 AI answers with citations, June–October 2026), when buyers do not know a brand yet AI names it in 3.9% of answers — and the brand’s own website is 0.7% of the sources AI reads. 99.3% comes from third parties. A provider that only works on your website is working on that 0.7%.

The five tests

  1. A measured Vietnamese baseline

    Ask for

    Answers for your category, buyers and competitors, in Vietnamese, per AI engine, with the sources each answer cites.

    What we provide

    A Day-0 baseline on a frozen question panel across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude and the live Google AI Overview page, with every citation recorded.

  2. Verifiable deliverables and metrics

    Ask for

    An agreed question set, measurement cadence, citation reports and staged targets. Decline promises of a “guaranteed AI recommendation”.

    What we provide

    Every rate is shown with its fraction. The question panel is held fixed so months compare. No score is edited by hand, and instrument faults are disclosed in the weekly report.

  3. A local source strategy

    Ask for

    Which Vietnamese outlets, industry sites and directories the provider will earn citations from — by name.

    What we provide

    We publish a monthly list of the sources AI engines cite in Vietnam and plan placements from it. In Vietnam the local media layer is being built with Focus Media Vietnam as first partner; other markets are on our roadmap.

  4. Cases with attribution

    Ask for

    Real cases that separate AI mentions, organic traffic and leads — not correlation sold as cause.

    What we provide

    Every published action is logged with its date and compared before and after on the same questions.

  5. A same-question test

    Ask for

    Two or three candidates audit or pilot the same question set, so methods and results can be compared.

    What we provide

    We offer it first: you receive our frozen question list with our baseline, and can ask any other candidate to report on the same list.

What we do not do

  • Guarantee rankings or AI recommendations.
  • Publish rankings that place ourselves first.
  • Buy placements in “best agency” lists.
