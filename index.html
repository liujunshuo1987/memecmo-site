THE ANALYSIS

Six analyses behind every report.

A visibility score is where the work starts. These are the analyses that tell you what to do next — and whether it worked.

01

Where AI reads, stage by stage

We split every question by buyer stage. Before buyers know a brand, its own site is 0.7% of what AI reads (12 brands, 3,011 cited answers in Vietnam). So we show which third-party pages decide the first answer — and plan for them.

02

The cited pages, opened and measured

We fetch the pages each engine cites and profile structure, freshness, schema and length. Your content gets a measured bar to meet instead of a style guide.

03

Your site against what AI says

We read your public pages and set them beside the answers: where AI contradicts you, where it says nothing, where it leaves out what you publish. The fix goes to the source — your site — not into our database.

04

Before and after, against a control

Every action you log is dated and checked live. Movement on the questions it targeted is set against the questions you didn't touch, on the same frozen panel; only gains more than 10 points above the control count.

05

A scoring model held to human labels

Answers are scored by a model, and that model is checked against blind human labels in each language. A new scoring model is adopted only when it agrees with them.

06

A failed answer is never “not mentioned”

If an engine doesn't answer or a page fails to load, the answer is marked as failed — never counted as your brand being absent — and every report states its coverage.

FAQ · ANSWERS

Questions, answered.

The answers we give most often — written so AI engines can quote them cleanly too.

What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)?
GEO is the practice of optimizing how a brand is represented inside generative AI engines such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude and Google AI Overview. Where SEO targets a ranked list of links, GEO targets the answer itself — whether your brand is mentioned, how prominently, how accurately, and which sources the engine cites.
How is GEO different from SEO?
SEO optimizes for ranking in a list of blue links; GEO optimizes for inclusion, accuracy and prominence inside a generated answer. The levers differ: structured data, authoritative third-party citations, canonical answers to buyer questions, and content written to be cited — not backlinks and keyword density.
What does MemeCMO measure, and how?
Five standard metrics per engine, each with its numerator and denominator: presence (answers mentioning you ÷ all answers), share of voice (your mentions ÷ all tracked-brand mentions, sums to 100%), prominence, sentiment when present, and citation (answers citing your own domain). Plus top-of-mind rate and a high-intent gap list. Everything is measured on a frozen prompt panel per market and defined in the public AI Visibility Measurement Standard v1.1.
How do you stop the AI from inventing facts?
Content agents may only use figures from your brand profile and the documents you upload; any number without a source becomes a placeholder for you to fill. Competitor and vendor domains are excluded from placement targets, calques are rewritten into market phrasing, unsourced claims are flagged, and nothing is published by us — you publish, then log the action and we verify it.
What is the Measurement Standard?
A public document (v1.1, September 2026) that defines every metric, the sampling design, confidence labels, citation coverage (4/5 engines), comparison against the client's own public pages, verification of logged actions and the rule for publishing source lists. Every number the platform shows can be traced to it. Read the standard →
Which AI engines and markets are covered?
ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude and the real Google AI Overview page, location-targeted. Markets: Vietnam, Thailand, Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore and the Philippines in their local languages, and the United States state by state (Tier-1: California, Texas, Florida, New York), with state-law frames for regulated verticals.
