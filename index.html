FAQ · ANSWERS

Questions, answered.

The answers we give most often — written so AI engines can quote them cleanly too.

What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)?
GEO is the practice of optimizing how a brand is represented inside generative AI engines such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude and Google AI Overview. Where SEO targets a ranked list of links, GEO targets the answer itself — whether your brand is mentioned, how prominently, how accurately, and which sources the engine cites.
How is GEO different from SEO?
SEO optimizes for ranking in a list of blue links; GEO optimizes for inclusion, accuracy and prominence inside a generated answer. The levers differ: structured data, authoritative third-party citations, canonical answers to buyer questions, and content written to be quoted — not backlinks and keyword density.
What does MemeCMO measure?
Standard metrics per engine: presence (how often you're mentioned), share of voice (your share of all brand mentions), citation rate (how often sources favoring you are cited), AI sentiment, top-of-mind rate (how often you're named first), and answer accuracy (whether AI's product facts and links match your verified standard answers). A weighted AI Mindset Index summarizes them; the metrics are the ground truth.
What is Answer Accuracy and why does it matter?
Answer Accuracy compares what AI actually tells your customers against your verified standard answers. When AI gives the wrong product facts, an outdated link or the wrong contact details, the customer acts on it and your support team pays. MemeCMO flags each inaccurate answer per engine so you can fix the source.
Which AI engines and markets are covered?
ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude and the real Google AI Overview page, location-targeted. Markets: Vietnam, Thailand, Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore and the Philippines in their local languages, and the United States state by state (Tier-1: California, Texas, Florida, New York) with state regulatory frames for regulated verticals.
How does pricing work?
Monthly plans per brand × market set the scan cadence, prompt library size, engines and reports; scheduled scans and reports are included. Credits cover on-demand scans and extra content runs. Agency partners get a workspace per client, rollup reports, unlimited seats and wholesale pricing.
Who is MemeCMO for?
Brands entering Southeast Asia — especially Chinese companies going global — regional brands defending their home market, US brands in regulated verticals where answers differ by state, and the agencies and media groups that serve them as channel partners.
How is MemeCMO different from Semrush, Profound or other GEO tools?
Most GEO tools stop at monitoring: they show you a dashboard and leave the work to you. MemeCMO's ten agents also produce the deliverables — standard answers, JSON-LD schema, gap content, third-party placements, encyclopedia entries — grounded in your verified brand facts. Two further differences: Answer Accuracy (we check whether AI gives your customers correct product facts, per engine — a support-cost metric monitoring-only tools don't have) and market depth where others are thin: Southeast Asian languages and US state-by-state measurement with state regulatory frames.
